    BantuanHelp

    Pertanyaan yang sering munculCommon questions

    Pil tidak muncul setelah dipasang.The pill doesn't show after installing.

    Pastikan Aksesibilitas Pilo menyala (Pilo → Lanjutan → Izin). Di HP Xiaomi, nyalakan juga Autostart dan atur baterai ke Tanpa batasan supaya tidak dimatikan sistem.Make sure Pilo's Accessibility is on (Pilo → Advanced → Permissions). On Xiaomi phones, also turn on Autostart and set battery to No restrictions so the system doesn't stop it.

    Pesan atau musik tidak muncul di pil.Messages or music don't appear in the pill.

    Nyalakan Akses notifikasi untuk Pilo. Di HyperOS namanya "Notification read, reply & control".Turn on Notification access for Pilo. On HyperOS it's called "Notification read, reply & control".

    Apakah Pilo bikin baterai boros?Does Pilo drain the battery?

    Saat diam, pil tidak menggambar apa pun. Kalau ingin lebih hemat, nyalakan Hemat baterai di Lanjutan: animasi terus-menerus berhenti dan maksimal 60 fps.When idle, the pill draws nothing. For extra savings, turn on Battery saver in Advanced: looping animations stop and it's capped at 60 fps.

    Aplikasi m-banking menolak jalan karena Aksesibilitas?My banking app refuses to run because of Accessibility?

    Aksesibilitas Pilo dibuat seminimal mungkin dan tidak membaca layar, jadi kebanyakan m-banking tetap jalan. Kalau ada yang menolak, laporkan nama aplikasinya.Pilo's Accessibility is as minimal as possible and doesn't read the screen, so most banking apps still run. If one refuses, report the app's name.

    Bagaimana cara mencopot Pilo?How do I uninstall Pilo?

    Tahan ikon Pilo, lalu Copot pemasangan. Semua pengaturan dan isi Rak ikut terhapus dari HP.Long-press the Pilo icon, then Uninstall. All settings and Shelf items are removed from your phone.
